Our Story

More than coffee.
It's a feeling.

Belle's Coffeehouse was born from a simple dream — to give Big Rapids a cozy, welcoming spot where you can slow down, connect, and enjoy something truly made with care. Founded by Belle Whipple, every cup is crafted with intention and every corner was designed to feel like home.
